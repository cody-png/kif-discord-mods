# Shiny Soul

Author unknown. Imported from the KIF Discord for KIF's Discord Mods catalog. This page describes version 1.0.1.

## What it does

- When one of your shiny Pokémon plays its shiny animation in battle, it first flashes in a colour you choose.
- Each flash ends with a quick pulse in size. The normal shiny sparkle plays right after it.
- You pick the colour, how light or dark it is, and how long the flash lasts.
- Only Pokémon on your side of the battle get the flash. Wild and opposing shinies show the normal animation only.

## Installing

Options > Mods > Browse > Discord Mods > Shiny Soul. It starts working the next time the game starts. If Mods is greyed out, set Options > KIF Settings > Modules > Mod Settings to On first.

## How to use it

- Go to Options > KIF Settings > Mods > Shiny Soul settings and press Shiny Soul Options. The same button is also under Settings by category > Interface.
- A menu called "Shiny Soul Mod Options:" opens. Each line shows its current choice, for example "Shiny Animations: ON" or "Select Base Color (Red)".
- Pick a line to change it, then choose Cancel to close the menu. Your choices are saved when the menu closes.
- The flash plays when you send out a shiny Pokémon, as long as the game shows battle animations.

## Settings

All in the Shiny Soul Mod Options menu. They apply to every save file and are kept after you close the game.

- **Shiny Animations:** ON or OFF. Default ON. Turns the colour flash on or off. The normal shiny sparkle still plays when it is OFF.
- **Select Base Color:** Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Teal, Blue, Purple, Pink, Cyan, Brown, Black, White or Grey. Default Red.
- **Select Intensity:** Light, Mid or Dark shade of that colour. Default Mid.
- **Select Animation Length:** Short (2 flashes), Medium (4 flashes) or Long (6 flashes). Default Medium.
- After a pick the game confirms it, for example "Selected base color: Blue." Choosing Cancel in a list keeps your old choice.

## Changes for KIF

- The original put its menu in PIF's own Options screen, where it never showed up in KIF. The menu is now under Options > KIF Settings > Mods.
- The original forgot your choices every time the game started. They are now kept.
- With KIF's Shiny Animation setting (Options > KIF Settings > Shinies) set to Off, the colour flash is skipped too.

## Limits

- With EBDX Battles On (Options > KIF Settings > Battles), the colour flash does not play. EBDX's own shiny animation plays instead.
- With KIF's Shiny Animation set to All, the extra animation on non-shiny Pokémon has no colour flash.
- The flash adds colour to the Pokémon, so Black gives only a faint light flash, not a dark one.
- Teal and Cyan look the same at Mid intensity.
- The flash has no sound. The sound the original plays is not among KIF's sound files.
