# Clothing Unlock

By Voidcat Cyr. Imported from the KIF Discord for KIF's Discord Mods catalog. This page describes version 1.0.1.

## What it does

- Opening the hat shop in a Poké Mart or in the Celadon Department Store unlocks every hat and every set of clothes in the game. This is the seller who asks "Would you like to purchase a hat?".
- The unlocks happen quietly: no message or question for each item.
- After that, the hat shop opens as usual, with that city's hats. Hats you own show a dash instead of a price.

## Installing

Options > Mods > Browse > Discord Mods > Clothing Unlock. It starts working the next time the game starts. If Mods is greyed out, set Options > KIF Settings > Modules > Mod Settings to On first.

## How to use it

- Open that hat shop once.
- Put on what you unlocked the usual way: Outfit in the pause menu, then Change hat or Change clothes.
- The new hats are also in the list of hats you can put on your Pokémon.
- The Clothes / Hats count on your Trainer Card goes up to match.

## Settings

- This mod has no settings.

## Changes for KIF

- No messages while it unlocks. The original showed a "You obtained a ..." message and a "Would you like to put it on right now?" question for every single hat and outfit.
- It skips one empty entry in the game's hat list. The original also gave you that entry, as a hat with no name.

## Limits

- Only that hat shop unlocks things. Other hat sellers, such as the stalls in Goldenrod Tunnel and the souvenir seller in Mt. Moon Square, do nothing extra. Neither do clothes shops or hair salons.
- Hairstyles are not unlocked.
- Opening the hat shop can take a few seconds longer, on every visit, because the mod goes through the whole list each time.
- The unlocks are saved in your save file. Removing the mod does not lock them again.
