# Romanoff's Rework

By Romanoff. Imported from the KIF Discord for KIF's Discord Mods catalog. This page describes version 1.0.1.

## What it does

- Rebalances 156 Pokémon: new types, base stats, EV yields, abilities, hidden abilities, level-up moves, and TM and tutor moves.
- Every other Pokémon stays as it is in KIF.
- Wild Pokémon, trainers' Pokémon and your own Pokémon all use the new data.
- Fusions made with these Pokémon use it too, since a fusion's types, stats and abilities come from its head and body.

## Installing

Options > Mods > Browse > Discord Mods > Romanoff's Rework. It starts working the next time the game starts. If Mods is greyed out, set Options > KIF Settings > Modules > Mod Settings to On first.

## How to use it

- There is nothing to turn on and no settings. Once installed, the changes are always in effect.
- To stop using it, set it to Off under Options > Mods > Installed, or pick it there and choose Uninstall. Either one takes effect the next time the game starts.
- Pokémon you already own:
  - Their types change right away.
  - Their stats are recalculated once each time the game starts, for your party and your PC boxes.
  - They usually keep the ability they have now until they evolve.
  - They keep the moves they know. New level-up moves are learned from the new lists as they level up.

## Changes for KIF

- The original loaded a full copy of the data for about 500 Pokémon and the triple fusions from KIF 0.19.8 (October 2024). That also put the old data back on about 370 of them that Romanoff never changed. This version changes only Romanoff's 156 Pokémon and leaves the rest as KIF has them.
- TM and tutor moves are added to each Pokémon's current list instead of replacing it.
- Pokémon you already own get their stats recalculated, so the new base stats show up without waiting for a level up.

## Limits

- It can't be used with KIF: Elite Redux, which changes the same Pokémon in its own way. While KIF: Elite Redux is On, this mod doesn't start and Options > Mods > Installed shows it as BLOCKED. Switch one of them Off.
- When the mod is off, types go back, but Pokémon you own keep their reworked stats until they level up, and an ability they got from this mod until they evolve.
- Pokémon in the Day Care are not part of the stat update. They get the new stats when they gain a level there.
