# Unlimited EV Gains

Author unknown. Imported from the KIF Discord for KIF's Discord Mods catalog. This page describes version 1.0.0.

## What it does

- Raises the EV limits for every Pokémon: 999 EVs in one stat (normally 252) and 5994 in all (normally 510). That is enough for 999 in all six stats.
- Pokémon keep gaining EVs from battles until they reach the new limits.
- Wings (Health Wing, Muscle Wing and the others) work up to 999 in their stat.
- Vitamins (Protein, Carbos and the others) still stop at 100 EVs in their stat, the same as without the mod.
- 4 EVs still give 1 stat point at level 100. So at level 100, 999 EVs add about 249 to a stat, where 252 EVs add about 63.
- Trainers' Pokémon get more EVs from level 58 up. Most trainers give their Pokémon 1.5 EVs per level in each stat. Without the mod this stops at 85; with it, a level 100 Pokémon has 150 in each stat. The level counted is usually the trainer's normal level, before the Hard difficulty or rematch level boosts.

## Installing

- Options > Mods > Browse > Discord Mods > Unlimited EV Gains.
- It starts working the next time the game starts.
- If Mods is greyed out, set Options > KIF Settings > Modules > Mod Settings to On first.

## How to use it

- There is nothing to turn on. Train as usual. Power items and Pokerus work as usual and count toward the new limits.
- To see a Pokémon's EVs, open its Summary and go to the Skills page. This needs KIF's Information module On (Options > KIF Settings > Modules) and its Summary IVs/EVs setting On (Options > KIF Settings > Graphics, under Screens). The setting is On by default.
- With KIF's EV Allocation setting On (Options > KIF Settings > Pokémon, under EVs and IVs; it shows when the Battles & Challenges module is On), EV points can be banked and spent on the Skills page up to the new limits: 999 in one stat and 5994 in all.

## Settings

- None. The mod has no settings of its own.

## Limits

- The limits can't be changed. To go back to 252 and 510, uninstall the mod.
- Removing the mod does not take EVs away. Pokémon keep their extra EVs and the stats they give.
- After removing the mod, a Pokémon with more than 252 EVs in any stat can cause an error when it gains EVs in a battle, or when you use that stat's Wing on it. Before you uninstall, bring every stat down to 252 or less. The EV-lowering berries (Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa and Tamato) take away 10 EVs each.
